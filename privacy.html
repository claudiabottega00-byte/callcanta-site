Privacy Policy

Canta App · Last updated October 1, 2026

Canta App ("Canta", "we") is an AI phone receptionist for small businesses. A business that uses Canta has it answer calls, take orders, bookings and messages, and tell the business what came in. This Privacy Policy covers the businesses that use Canta and the people who call them.

What we collect

  • From businesses: the account holder's name and email, business details (hours, services, menu, policies), the mobile numbers and email addresses they ask us to notify, and connections they choose to make, such as Google Calendar, Google Sheets or Square.
  • From callers: the phone number they call from, what is said on the call (as a written transcript), and what they give the receptionist, such as a name, an order, a booking or a message.
  • For text messages: the mobile number, the fact and time of the opt-in, and the messages sent.

How we use it

  • To answer calls for the business and pass on what callers asked for.
  • To notify the business by email, text message or in the app.
  • To send a caller something they asked for on the call, such as a link to pay for their order.
  • To keep the service working, fix problems and prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

A caller's details go to the business they called. We use service providers to run Canta App (phone and text delivery, voice, AI processing, payments and email), and they handle data only to provide those services to us.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messages

Canta App sends text messages only to people who opted in: business owners and staff whose numbers were entered in the app, and callers who agreed on a call to receive a payment link. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. See the SMS Terms in our Terms of Service.

Keeping and deleting

A business can delete its receptionists, calls, customers and messages in the app at any time. To ask us to access or delete your information, write to hello@callcanta.com.
