What it does

  • Answers questionsHours, location, prices, your menu or services, in English, Spanish, Mandarin and Vietnamese. It only says what you've told it.
  • Takes what callers wantPhone orders, appointments and reservations, messages and new-customer details, read back to the caller before they're saved.
  • Hands off to a personWhen a caller needs someone on your team, it transfers the call to the number you choose.
  • Tells you right awayEverything lands in the Canta app, with an email after each call and, if you want, a text to you and your team.

About our text messages

Canta App sends two kinds of text message, and never marketing. A business and its team get an alert when the receptionist takes a new order, booking, message or lead; they sign up by entering their mobile numbers in the Canta app. A caller who places a phone order can get one text with a link to pay, only after saying yes when the receptionist asks on the call.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.
