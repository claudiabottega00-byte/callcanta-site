  • Don't use Canta for unlawful, deceptive or harassing calls or messages, or to send texts to people who haven't agreed to them.

SMS Terms

  • Program: Canta App sends transactional text messages. (1) Alerts to a business owner and their staff when the receptionist takes a new order, booking, message or lead. (2) A single payment link to a caller who has just placed an order by phone. No marketing messages are sent.
  • Opting in: a business opts in a mobile number by entering it in the Canta app under Setup, Notifications. A caller opts in by saying yes when the receptionist asks, on the call, whether it may text a link to pay.
  • Message frequency: alerts vary with how many calls the business receives. A caller receives one message per order.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opting out and help: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, and HELP for help. You can also write to hello@callcanta.com.
